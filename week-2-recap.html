04Predictions check-in

Four of the League Preview's predictions moved this week, two in the right direction and two in the wrong one.

Halfway there

Two of the four re-drafted 2024 Raj players are dropped again before Week 10. Matt A. cut Jaylen Wright on September 18, the second time Wright has been dropped since Raj took him with the draft's final pick. Raj dropped him the same night he drafted him. One more of MarShawn Lloyd, Tank Bigsby and Keaton Mitchell has to go before Week 10.

Wrong
