The departed

Three guys have had a seat here and given it back.

NORCROSS NINNIES

Kevin Sprague

Two years here, one of them on ESPN. In his Sleeper year he went 4-10 and scored 1,514 points, the fewest in the league. He was the only team under 1,550. Then he was gone.

He still made five trades that season, which is more than Malcolm has made in three years.

THE ESPN YEARS

Blake and the other Kevin

Blake left. The other Kevin left too, but not before spending most of a draft day in the bathroom.

He still hit on more picks that day than Ward did in the entire 2024 draft.

2024 TO NOW

Jonathan Pye

He took Kevin's seat and has been better in it. He went 8-6 and made the playoffs in 2025 while facing the fewest points in the league.

Then he traded Jaxon Smith-Njigba to his brother.

THE PATTERN

It keeps working out

Every guy who has left this league was near the bottom when he left. Every guy who replaced him has been better.

Malcolm and Brandon, this is probably not about you.

Year one

Six trades before the season even started. By week 8 he had added Tyreek Hill, Jonathan Taylor, Sam LaPorta, Chris Olave, DK Metcalf and Breece Hall, all of it paid for with picks that were somebody else's problem later.

Then he won it and put it in the team name.

The bill came

2024 showed up, the picks were gone, and he went 6-8 with a team named "Ill always have last year." Then he rebuilt and won again in 2025.

Everybody watched that and decided it works. There were 19 trades last season, the most we have ever had.

The trophy case

The Traded And Won The League Cup
Raj

He is in 25 of the league's 56 trades and has two rings. He told everybody what he was doing in the team name and did it anyway.

Always A Bridesmaid
Jordan Pye

He has played in two finals and lost both to the same guy.

Best Team, Wrong League
Luke

He is 29-13, the best record we have, and the luckiest man in the league by almost five expected wins. His actual ring came from the ESPN days, which barely counts.

Comeback Of The Century
Reynolds

He went from 2-12 to champion in one year, then went 12-2 and missed the final.

Perpetual Mediocrity
Matt

He has been the highest scorer in five weeks and the lowest in five more, and he named his own team after it.

The Sandbag
David

He has given up 5,702.90 points, the most in league history and 77 more than Malcolm, the next closest. He is still 5-0 against Jeremy.

Thanks For Coming
Brandon

He is 12-30 and has never made the playoffs, with an 0-5 record against Luke and 0-4 against Creger. Still brings up his ESPN ring, and honestly, it's the only thing he's got.

Do Not Disturb
Malcolm

He has made three trades in three years, and two of them are on this page. Nobody can reach him, and it might be for his own good.

So here we are

Raj has won two of the last three and the league is named after him again. Jordan and Luke have been great for three straight years and have nothing. Reynolds just went 12-2 and lost. Creger keeps winning with a running back none of you wanted. And every one of you is about to draft like this never happened.
