04The departed

Three guys have had a seat here and given it back.

NORCROSS NINNIES

Kevin Sprague

Two years here, one of them on ESPN. In his Sleeper year he went 4-10 and scored 1,514 points, the fewest in the league. He was the only team under 1,550. Then he was gone.

He still made five trades that season, which is more than Malcolm has made in three years.

THE ESPN YEARS

Blake and the other Kevin

Blake left. The other Kevin left too, but not before spending most of a draft day in the bathroom.

He still hit on more picks that day than Ward did in the entire 2024 draft.

2024 TO NOW

Jonathan Pye

Took Kevin's seat and has been better in it. Went 8-6 and made the playoffs in 2025 while giving up the fewest points in the league.

Then he traded Jaxon Smith-Njigba to his brother.

THE PATTERN

It keeps working out

Every guy who has left this league was near the bottom when he left. Every guy who replaced him has been better.

Malcolm and Brandon, this is not about you. Probably.
