Dynasty Power Rankings

Raj and Reynolds have combined to win three championships running, and heading into 2026 they also own the two best rosters in the building. The other ten of you are still drafting like that is about to change.

1
Raj 9-5 in 2025 1,839 PROJ A- DRAFT

He posted the best draft grade in the league, and his starting lineup projects for 1,839 points, built around Jonathan Taylor and a bench so deep at quarterback that Lamar Jackson and Matthew Stafford are both stuck on it. He already has two Sleeper titles, and this roster looks built for a third.

2
Reynolds 12-2 in 2025 1,977 PROJ B DRAFT

His projected lineup is the best in the league at 1,977 points, stacked with Bijan Robinson, Saquon Barkley, Puka Nacua and Ja'Marr Chase — he sold this year's picks and kept every player that actually matters. Twelve wins on a five-pick draft was never about the draft.

3
Creger 11-3 in 2025 1,679 PROJ B+ DRAFT

Joe Burrow and Justin Jefferson headline a lineup that projects for 1,679 points, and he still found room to sell Alvin Kamara for three thirds and draft him back in the fifth round. He has eleven wins, the second-best draft grade in the league, and a roster with no obvious hole in it.

4
Jordan 10-4 in 2025 1,802 PROJ C+ DRAFT

Christian McCaffrey and Jaxon Smith-Njigba anchor a lineup that projects for 1,802 points, third-best in the league, with Travis Kelce as the only tight end worth starting. The record is elite, the draft grade is not, and the roster somehow makes both of those true at once.

5
Ward 3-11 in 2025 1,586 PROJ B- DRAFT

He has two starting-caliber quarterbacks in Brock Purdy and Jaxson Dart and three real receivers behind them, and the lineup projects competitively even before the draft grade and the youngest roster in the league get counted. The rebuild is finally showing up somewhere other than the standings.

6
David 5-9 in 2025 1,638 PROJ C DRAFT

Trevor Lawrence and Jayden Daniels give him two real starting quarterbacks, and James Cook anchors a lineup that projects for 1,638 points despite the five-win season that produced it. The rebuild drafted correctly, and the roster is already ahead of where the record says it should be.

7
Luke 8-6 in 2025 1,725 PROJ C+ DRAFT

Amon-Ra St. Brown and Tee Higgins give him the second-deepest receiver room in the league, behind only Reynolds, and the lineup still projects for 1,725 points on a team that has no Sleeper ring to show for any of it. The best regular season record in league history keeps finding new ways to mean nothing in December.

8
Jonathan 8-6 in 2025 1,647 PROJ C- DRAFT

Jahmyr Gibbs projects for more points than every quarterback in the league, every other running back, and everyone else too — the single highest-projected player at any position, on any roster. Jonathan built almost nothing around him: Caleb Williams at quarterback, Drake London at receiver, and a tight end room that could not crack 85 points combined.

9
Brandon 2-12 in 2025 1,622 PROJ C DRAFT

Nico Collins and a two-deep at running back in Breece Hall and Omarion Hampton give him more roster than a 2-12 season usually buys. He sold the first overall pick seven minutes into the draft and still climbed three spots on talent alone.

10
Jeremy 6-8 in 2025 1,514 PROJ C- DRAFT

Josh Allen and Drake Maye are both on this roster, which means one of the league's best quarterbacks is stuck on his bench behind an even better one, while the receiver room behind Davante Adams thins out fast. Two elite quarterbacks and one starting lineup is not a plan; it is a hoarding problem.

11
Matt A. 5-9 in 2025 1,704 PROJ D+ DRAFT

CeeDee Lamb and a Chase Brown-Ashton Jeanty backfield give him a lineup that projects better than seven other teams in the league, despite the worst draft grade this side of Malcolm. The roster is better than the grade and better than the record — somebody just has to find a way to use it.

12
Malcolm 5-9 in 2025 1,507 PROJ D+ DRAFT

Jared Goff, Patrick Mahomes and Bryce Young are all on this roster — three real quarterbacks fighting for one starting lineup — and it is still the worst projected lineup in the league. The oldest roster in the league is also the least productive one, and the quarterback room is not the part that needed depth.

Three teams move up once the lineup counts: Raj from third to first, Brandon from last to ninth, and Ward from seventh to fifth. Six teams slide and three hold exactly where they were — a great draft grade or a great 2025 record turns out to mean less than an actual healthy roster.
Team by team

Three of Raj's 2024 busts ended up on other rosters this year, and Alvin Kamara changed hands three times in ten months without settling anywhere.

#1Raj
9-5 in 2025 · A- draft

Three of his 2024 castoffs ended up on other rosters this year — MarShawn Lloyd to Creger, Tank Bigsby to Jordan, Keaton Mitchell to Brandon — and he closed this draft by taking the fourth one, Jaylen Wright, back for himself.

The empire runs on buying, selling and occasionally re-buying its own mistakes, and it keeps winning anyway. Nobody else in this league has figured out how to turn their own draft busts into someone else's headache and still come out ahead twice.

#2Reynolds
12-2 in 2025 · B draft

He has sold future draft capital three years running and still has the best-projected roster in the league, which is either generational team management or a bill that has not come due yet.

Selling picks and winning is supposed to catch up with somebody eventually. It has not caught up with him yet, and nothing here suggests this is the year it starts.

#3Creger
11-3 in 2025 · B+ draft

He also picked up MarShawn Lloyd, one of the players Raj gave up on in 2024 — Raj's castoffs keep finding better homes than Raj found for them.

He has eleven wins, eight picks, four of them veterans, and a roster built to win now instead of later. Finding value in somebody else's mistake is apparently just how he operates.

#4Jordan
10-4 in 2025 · C+ draft

He also took Tank Bigsby, one of Raj's discarded 2024 rookies, closing a loop that now runs through three different rosters.

The roster is good; the patience is worse than the record suggests he can afford. He has the second-best record in league history, and he still can't leave a receiver on the board.

#5Ward
3-11 in 2025 · B- draft

He bought first-round picks off Raj and Reynolds and built the youngest roster in the league around them, for a team that just went 3-11 — which is exactly the plan a 3-11 team should be running.

He also drafted a quarterback, a defense and a kicker for the third straight year, and reached thirty-five slots for Germie Bernard along the way. The plan is exactly right; the habits underneath it have not changed at all.

#6David
5-9 in 2025 · C draft

He built five rookies and no veterans on a roster that won five games last year, the textbook rebuild move.

He also owned Alvin Kamara for four days this August, the middle stop between Creger selling him and Luke giving him back. Somewhere between correct roster theory and Kamara changing hands three times in a month, this team is still trying to figure out which one it actually is.

#7Luke
8-6 in 2025 · C+ draft

Five picks were left after he sold his second, third and fourth, and bought Matt's first — a thin return for a manager who has never needed volume to be good.

The only ring in his trophy case is from the ESPN years, and this league has never fully agreed to count it. Everything since has been the same story: excellent record, empty hands.

#8Jonathan
8-6 in 2025 · C- draft

He traded his second to David and his third to Creger before the draft even started, which is one way to explain how he ended up with the fewest picks in the league.

He made the playoffs in 2025 on the back of a defense that gave up the fewest points in the league, and this draft did not add anything to build on it.

#9Brandon
2-12 in 2025 · C draft

He also picked up Keaton Mitchell, another name off Raj's 2024 discard pile — the fourth different roster this year to go shopping in Raj's trash.

Selling the top overall pick also bought him a 2028 first, a 2027 second and a 2027 third to go with the players he kept. The worst season in the league somehow ended with more future capital than most of the teams that beat him.

#10Jeremy
6-8 in 2025 · C- draft

He drafted no running back and no quarterback. A kicker and a defense ate two of his five picks, and the three players left include exactly one rookie.

There is no timeline here to evaluate, because there is no plan. A 6-8 team needs a direction, and this draft did not pick one.

#11Matt A.
5-9 in 2025 · D+ draft

He named the team Perpetual Mediocrity in 2024, and every season since has doubled as research into whether that name undersells him.

Five wins and a draft grade to match — the trademark is holding up fine without any help from him trying.

#12Malcolm
5-9 in 2025 · D+ draft

He traded a 2027 second for Deebo Samuel, age 30, three days before a rookie draft — the fourth trade of his career in four years — then drafted exactly one rookie in seven picks.

He also took a quarterback in the fourth round, a year after taking one in the first round, which is the only sign of growth on this card. The oldest roster in the league did not get here by accident.
