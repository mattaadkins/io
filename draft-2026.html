01The grades

Four things get measured. Value is where you took a guy against where the board had him. Timeline is whether the ages you drafted match the record you finished with. Capital is how many picks you brought and whether you earned them. Construction is whether the six players you added form a roster or a list.

03Reaches and steals

Kickers and defenses are excluded here, because Sleeper rates them generously and all twelve of you correctly ignored that. The rest of you have no such excuse.

BIGGEST REACH OF THE DRAFT

Ward takes Germie Bernard at 14

The board had him 49th. That is a thirty-five slot leap in a draft that is only seventy-two picks long. He was going to be there in the fourth round. He was going to be there in the fifth.

STEAL OF THE DRAFT

Creger takes Alvin Kamara at 57

Board had him sixteenth. Eleven teams passed on a starting running back to draft players they will cut in September. Creger is 11-3 and just added a starter with a fifth-round pick.

THE OTHER REACHES

Jordan +33, David +32, Creger +26

Malachi Fields at 20, Zachariah Branch at 16, Chris Bell at 28. Three receivers who were not going anywhere. Somebody should start a group chat about patience.

THE OTHER STEALS

Reynolds -21, Malcolm -21, Raj -24

Malik Willis at 35, Bryce Young at 39, Eli Stowers at 48. Quarterbacks fall in a one quarterback league, which everybody knows and nobody acts on until round four.

04Whose pick was it anyway

Eighteen of the seventy-two picks had already been traded at least once, some of them twice. Four teams drafted mostly with other people's capital. One team drafted with almost none of its own.

THE PICK THAT DECIDED THE DRAFT

Brandon earned 1.01 and gave it away

Two wins and twelve losses bought him the first overall pick. He traded it to Raj, who used it on Jeremiyah Love, the top player on the board. The worst season in the league produced the best pick in the draft, for somebody else.

BUYING WHILE LOSING

Ward drafted eight times

He bought first-round picks from Raj and from Reynolds. Three wins last year, most picks this year, youngest class in the league at 21.8. The plan is right even when the picks are not.

THE THIRD ROUND WAS SPONSORED BY

Creger, who bought three of them

Jonathan's third, Matt's third and David's third all ended up with Creger. He turned them into MarShawn Lloyd, Chris Bell and Cyrus Allen, which is a sentence that will either age beautifully or not at all.

FEWEST SWINGS

Jonathan, four picks

He traded his second to David and his third to Creger and showed up with four selections. One of them was a kicker. Reynolds also came in light with five, but Reynolds has a ring and a system. Jonathan has four picks.

09So that happened

Seventy-two picks, eighteen of them borrowed, four of them spent on players Raj already gave up on once. The best team in the league drafted five times. The worst team in the league earned the first pick and sold it. Ward bought everything and reached on most of it, and he still had a better plan than half of you.
