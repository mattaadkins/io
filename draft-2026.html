Dynasty Power Rankings

Raj and Reynolds have won the last three titles between them, and they own the two best rosters going into 2026.

1
Raj 9-5 in 2025 1,839 PROJ A- DRAFT

He posted the best draft grade in the league, and his starting lineup projects for 1,839 points around Jonathan Taylor. He is so deep at quarterback that Lamar Jackson and Matthew Stafford are both stuck on his bench.

2
Reynolds 12-2 in 2025 1,977 PROJ B DRAFT

His projected lineup is the best in the league at 1,977 points, with Bijan Robinson, Saquon Barkley, Puka Nacua and Ja'Marr Chase in it.

3
Creger 11-3 in 2025 1,679 PROJ B+ DRAFT

Joe Burrow and Justin Jefferson headline a lineup that projects for 1,679 points, and he posted the second-best draft grade in the league.

4
Jordan 10-4 in 2025 1,802 PROJ C+ DRAFT

Christian McCaffrey and Jaxon Smith-Njigba anchor a lineup that projects for 1,802 points, third-best in the league, with Travis Kelce at tight end.

5
Ward 3-11 in 2025 1,586 PROJ B- DRAFT

He has two starting-caliber quarterbacks in Brock Purdy and Jaxson Dart and three real receivers behind them, coming off a 3-11 season.

6

Trevor Lawrence and Jayden Daniels give him two starting quarterbacks, and James Cook anchors a lineup that projects for 1,638 points coming off a 5-9 season.

7

Amon-Ra St. Brown and Tee Higgins give him the second-deepest receiver room in the league, behind only Reynolds, and the lineup projects for 1,725 points.

9

Matt A. 5-9 in 2025 1,704 PROJ D+ DRAFT

CeeDee Lamb and a Chase Brown-Ashton Jeanty backfield give him the fifth-best projected lineup in the league, even with a D+ draft.

Team by team

Three of Raj's 2024 busts ended up on other rosters this year, and Alvin Kamara went from Creger to David to Luke and back to Creger in ten months.

#1Raj
9-5 in 2025 · A- draft

Three of his 2024 castoffs ended up on other rosters this year — MarShawn Lloyd to Creger, Tank Bigsby to Jordan, Keaton Mitchell to Brandon — and he closed this draft by taking the fourth one, Jaylen Wright, back for himself.

He has made more trades than anyone in the league, and he has two of the three Sleeper titles.

#2Reynolds
12-2 in 2025 · B draft

He has sold future draft capital three years running and still has the best-projected roster in the league.

He went from 2-12 to champion in 2024 on a roster built mostly out of trades with Raj, then went 12-2 last year.

#3Creger
11-3 in 2025 · B+ draft

He drafted MarShawn Lloyd, one of the players Raj gave up on in 2024.

He has made the playoffs in all three Sleeper seasons, one of only three managers who can say that.

#4Jordan
10-4 in 2025 · C+ draft

He drafted Tank Bigsby, another of Raj's 2024 rookies.

He is tied for the second-best record in league history and has lost two finals, both to Raj.

#5Ward
3-11 in 2025 · B- draft

He bought Raj's and Reynolds' first-round picks after going 3-11 and used them on Nicholas Singleton and Fernando Mendoza.

He also drafted a quarterback, a defense and a kicker for the third straight year, and reached thirty-five slots for Germie Bernard along the way.

#6David
5-9 in 2025 · C draft

He turned Alvin Kamara and Harold Fannin into Luke's 2027 first-round pick in August.

His 2024 draft, with Brock Bowers, Jayden Daniels and Khalil Shakir, is still the best anybody has had here.

#7Luke
8-6 in 2025 · C+ draft

The first-round pick he bought from Matt came in the same August trade that brought him Dak Prescott and DK Metcalf for C.J. Stroud and Ladd McConkey.

His only ring is from the ESPN years. He has the best record of the Sleeper era and has never played in a Sleeper final.

#8Jonathan
8-6 in 2025 · C- draft

He traded his second to David and his third to Creger before the draft started.

He went 8-6 and made the playoffs in 2025 while facing the fewest points in the league.

#9Brandon
2-12 in 2025 · C draft

He drafted Keaton Mitchell, one more of Raj's 2024 rookies.

The trade that sent the first overall pick and Rashee Rice to Raj brought back Nico Collins, Breece Hall, a 2028 first, a 2027 second and a 2027 third.

#10Jeremy
6-8 in 2025 · C- draft

He sent a second-round pick to Luke for Jakobi Meyers the day before the draft.

He went 6-8 last year and is still 0-5 against David.

#11Matt A.
5-9 in 2025 · D+ draft

He named the team Perpetual Mediocrity in 2024. He has been the league's top scorer in five weeks and its lowest scorer in five more.

He traded the fifth overall pick to Luke before the draft, so his first selection came in the second round.

#12Malcolm
5-9 in 2025 · D+ draft

He took Bryce Young in the fourth round, a year after taking Cam Ward in the first.

He now has four quarterbacks and one spot to start them in.

Malcolm shows up in two of the twelve.

Jeremiyah Love finishes as a top-twelve back and Brandon has to hear about it every week.

Ward's eight picks produce exactly one starter.
Creger trades Alvin Kamara again before Week 8 for more than a fifth-round pick.
Malcolm misses the playoffs for the fourth straight season and makes his fifth career trade in 2027.

Jordan finishes higher than #4 by the bye week and lower than it by the final. He always does.
Malcolm's #12 power rank holds all season, and the three quarterbacks sitting on his bench never become the reason it doesn't.
