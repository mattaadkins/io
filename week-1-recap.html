The two teams the Dynasty Power Rankings crowned #1 and #2 this preseason played each other in Week 1, and it was not competitive. Raj won by fifty points.

04Predictions check-in

Five of the League Preview's twelve predictions already have real signal. One of them is already looking shaky.

Trending right

Two of the four re-drafted 2024 Raj players are dropped again before Week 10. All four had rough weeks — MarShawn Lloyd (3.7), Tank Bigsby (0.3), Keaton Mitchell (0.9) and Jaylen Wright, who finished with a negative score. Two of them getting dropped before Week 10 is now the safe bet, not the bold one.

Rough start

Jahmyr Gibbs outscores every quarterback in the league this season. Josh Allen outscored him in Week 1, 39.7 to 33.6, under this league's own scoring. The season-long claim is not dead — Gibbs still projects as the best player in the league the rest of the way — but the very first week did not cooperate.

Trending right

Jordan finishes higher than #4 by the bye week and lower than it by the final. Jordan already sits above his preseason #4 slot, up to third in the Season Power Rankings after one week. The bye-week half of this one is already trending true; the back half, about finishing lower by the final, still has all season to happen.

On track

Malcolm's #12 power rank holds all season, and the three quarterbacks on his bench never become the reason it doesn't. Malcolm is still last, in both power rankings, after Week 1. His cheapest quarterback, Bryce Young, outscored both Jared Goff and Patrick Mahomes anyway — the sign of growth on his card just keeps signing.

Too early

Raj's #1 power rank means less than his two rings do. Raj's Week 1 was won by Jonathan Taylor and a stacked receiving corps, not by Jeremiyah Love, who managed a modest 13.0 points. The team is still #1. The specific player behind the joke has not shown up yet.

05Bench regret

One lineup call was worth more than the final margin in five of this week's six games.

Reynolds started Jaylen Waddle over Christian Watson in the Raj game. Waddle scored 1.2 points. Watson, sitting on the bench, scored 32.7 — the single biggest points-left-on-the-bench gap in the league this week. Even with that swing Raj still wins, but the final margin drops from fifty points to eighteen, which is at least a version of this loss Reynolds could tell people about.
