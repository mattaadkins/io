Predictions check-in

Five of the League Preview's twelve predictions moved in Week 1, and one of them is in trouble.

Trending right

Two of the four re-drafted 2024 Raj players are dropped again before Week 10. All four had rough weeks — MarShawn Lloyd (3.7), Tank Bigsby (0.3), Keaton Mitchell (0.9) and Jaylen Wright, who finished with a negative score.

Rough start

Jahmyr Gibbs outscores every quarterback in the league this season. Josh Allen outscored him in Week 1, 39.7 to 33.6, under this league's own scoring. It's a season-long call, and Gibbs still projects as the best player in the league the rest of the way.

Trending right

Jordan finishes higher than #4 by the bye week and lower than it by the final. Jordan is up to third in the Season Power Rankings, above his preseason #4. The second half, finishing below #4, has all season to happen.

On track

Malcolm's #12 power rank holds all season, and the three quarterbacks on his bench never become the reason it doesn't. Malcolm is last in both power rankings. His cheapest quarterback, Bryce Young, outscored both Jared Goff and Patrick Mahomes.

Too early

Raj's #1 power rank means less than his two rings do. Jonathan Taylor and the receivers won Raj's Week 1. Jeremiyah Love scored 13.0.

Bench regret

Reynolds made the worst lineup call of the week.

Reynolds started Jaylen Waddle over Christian Watson in the Raj game. Waddle scored 1.2 points. Watson, on the bench, scored 32.7, the biggest bench miss in the league this week. Raj still wins with Watson in the lineup, but by about nineteen instead of fifty.
