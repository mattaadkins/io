Front page

Ward starts buying

Is he turning the corner?

On Monday, Ward sent a 2027 first-round pick to Reynolds for Christian Watson, a day after Watson caught six passes for 143 yards and two touchdowns on Reynolds' bench. It was the first time since August 2023 that Ward has traded a first-round pick for a player.

A year ago this month, Ward was selling. He made five trades in October 2025, sent out George Kittle, Nico Collins, Josh Jacobs, Jaylen Waddle, Kimani Vidal and Travis Kelce, and came back with four first-round picks. He finished 3-11.

This season he has been buying. In August he traded third-round picks for Jake Ferguson and Kyle Monangai. The first-round pick he paid for Watson is the one Creger sent him for Jacobs last October.

Ward set the only perfect lineup of the season in Week 4, and he is a 24.8-point favorite over Jonathan in Week 6, the biggest favorite he has been all season. He still owns 13 picks in the 2027 and 2028 drafts, tied for the most in the league.

Game of the week

Reynolds set the season high and handed Luke his first loss.

Reynolds193.28
Luke126.54

Reynolds' 193.28 beat Jeremy's 193.02 from Week 2 by 0.26 for the highest score of the season, and the 66.74-point margin is the second-largest of the year. He had already won before Puka Nacua caught 17 passes for 138 yards and a touchdown on Monday night.

For the first time this season, Reynolds started the best quarterback on his roster. Jacoby Brissett threw for 292 yards and three touchdowns for 31.28, more than Kyler Murray or Kirk Cousins scored. Reynolds claimed Daniel Jones off waivers on Wednesday anyway, and he now has four quarterbacks.

Dak Prescott threw two interceptions for Luke on Thursday and scored 14.64, and Tee Higgins and Harold Fannin combined for 5.6.

A second-round habit. Reynolds keeps paying second-round picks for backup running backs at the height of their fame. Last October, Kimani Vidal ran for 124 yards and caught a touchdown, and four days later Reynolds traded his own 2027 second to Ward for him. Vidal started the next five games for Reynolds, scored 51.2 points in them and was cut in December. This month, Saquon Barkley played four snaps in Week 4 and has not played since. Matt A. claimed Will Shipley off waivers that Wednesday, and two days later Reynolds sent him Creger's 2027 second for Shipley and a 2028 third. Shipley scored a touchdown on 13 snaps in Week 5 and is listed Doubtful with a foot injury.
