Game of the week

Reynolds led by 57.90 after Thursday, and Jordan had cut it to 16.06 before Monday night.

Reynolds158.12
Jordan145.68

Reynolds got 35.3 from Bijan Robinson, who ran for 194 yards and two touchdowns on Thursday, and 22.6 from Christian Watson, so he led 57.90 to 0 before Jordan had a starter on the field. Jordan then outscored him 133.06 to 91.22 on Sunday and went into Monday down 16.06.

Jalen Hurts for Jordan and Saquon Barkley for Reynolds were the last two starters, both in the Eagles game, and Jordan needed Hurts to outscore Barkley by more than sixteen. Hurts was projected for 20.0 and finished with 12.62 on 153 passing yards, an interception and a rushing touchdown. Barkley scored 9.0, so Jordan made up 3.62 of the 16.

Reynolds' 158.12 was the highest score of Week 3 and his first win of the season. Jordan lost with the fourth-highest score of the week, and Jaxon Smith-Njigba scored 35.36 for him on ten catches, 128 yards and two touchdowns.
